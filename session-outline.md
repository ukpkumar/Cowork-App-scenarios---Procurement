## Key messages

- Chat answers questions. Cowork does the work: it plans, builds files and apps, sends only after approval, and runs on a schedule or trigger.
- The App skill (/app) turns a plain-language description into a working interactive app with a live preview.
- How an app gets built: Describe → Review the plan and data access → Preview, test and refine → Share after IT review.
- Cowork drafts. People decide.

## Value for the procurement team (qualitative)

- Shorter cycle time from bid receipt to award. [Target cycle-time reduction]
- Fewer missed renewals: notice periods and auto-renewals flagged early. [Target renewal capture]
- Consistent evaluations: the same checks and weighting on every tender, with evidence cited.
- Spend visibility: category, supplier and off-contract spend in one view.
- Audit-ready records: plans, citations and approvals logged.
- Time back for negotiation and supplier relationships.
